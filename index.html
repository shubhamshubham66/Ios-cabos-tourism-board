About Los Cabos

Where Desert Meets Ocean. And Every Wedding Feels Extraordinary.

Few destinations offer the diversity of Los Cabos. Towering desert landscapes, turquoise coastlines, luxury resorts, private villas, championship golf courses, and dramatic oceanfront settings come together to create one of the most exclusive wedding destinations in Mexico.

Whether you’re planning an intimate celebration or a multi-day South Asian wedding with hundreds of guests, Los Cabos offers breathtaking venues, unforgettable experiences, and seamless hospitality designed for every generation attending your celebration.

Destinations

From Iconic Venues to Hidden Gems

Cabo San Lucas
Home to luxury resorts, exclusive wedding venues, vibrant nightlife, and the world-famous El Arco, Cabo San Lucas is the heart of destination celebrations.
San José del Cabo
A sophisticated colonial town known for charming courtyards, art galleries, luxury boutiques, and intimate wedding settings full of character and elegance.
El Arco
One of Mexico’s most recognizable natural landmarks and an unforgettable backdrop for wedding photography, yacht experiences, and romantic celebrations.
Cabo Pulmo
A protected marine paradise offering snorkeling, diving, and eco-luxury experiences for couples seeking adventure beyond the wedding festivities.
Todos Santos
A stylish desert escape blending art, culture, boutique luxury, and relaxed sophistication perfect for intimate wedding experiences.
